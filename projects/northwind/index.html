About this concept

Who it’s for

Northwind is designed first for the person who has to explain the footprint, not only the person who collects the data.

  • CFO & finance leadA number they can defend, and a sense of what a cut is worth.
  • Sustainability / ESG managerWhere to focus, and a clear way to show progress.
  • Procurement & supply chainWhich suppliers drive emissions, so they know whom to push.
  • Executives & boardThe headline in ten seconds, with no spreadsheet.
  • Consultants & auditorsA view they can walk a client through.

Why now: reporting rules such as the EU’s CSRD are pushing companies to measure supply-chain (Scope 3) emissions, and large customers increasingly ask their suppliers for the numbers.

A design concept with invented sample data. Carbon accounting is an established category with real products; this explores a clearer, more visual way to communicate the result.
