Evenly, banking for people who invoice.

Freelance income arrives in lumps; rent doesn't. Evenly smooths the gap — a concept you can click through. Everything runs in your browser with sample data.

  • Invoice from the app, get paid into it
  • Tax pot fills itself from every payment
  • One number: what's safe to spend
  • Pay yourself a steady salary
A design exercise, not a real bank. Freelancer banking is a crowded space (Found, Tide, Lili and others) — this concept explores a calmer, one-number take.
