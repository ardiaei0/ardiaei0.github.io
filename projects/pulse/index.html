Pulse, a calmer way to recover.

Rehab exercises are easy to forget once you’re home. Pulse turns a recovery plan into a few gentle daily steps, with a 10-second check-in so progress is visible.

  • Plans for ten common injuries, set up in three taps
  • A guided timer for every set
  • A daily check-in: pain, stiffness, swelling, sleep
  • Progress charts that show you’re improving
A design concept with sample data — not medical advice. A real plan comes from your physiotherapist.
